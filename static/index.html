ABOUT AUTO VIBE

Welcome to AUTO VIBE - Narikknui's first premier automotive detailing destination. We're not just detailers; we're automotive perfectionists obsessed with bringing out the absolute best in every vehicle that enters our facility.

With cutting-edge technology, premium-grade products, and a team of skilled detailing specialists, we deliver results that exceed expectations. From exotic supercars to daily drivers, every vehicle receives the same meticulous attention to detail.

Our state-of-the-art facility in Narikknui features climate-controlled bays, advanced coating systems, and the latest detailing equipment. We use only the finest ceramic, graphene, and polymer coatings backed by industry-leading warranties.

CERAMIC COATING

Ultimate protection with 9H hardness nano-ceramic coating. Creates a permanent molecular bond with your paint, providing unmatched scratch resistance, UV protection, and hydrophobic properties. Lasts 3-5 years with proper maintenance.

  • ✓ 9H Scratch Resistance
  • ✓ Extreme Hydrophobic Effect
  • ✓ UV & Chemical Protection
  • ✓ 3-5 Year Durability
💎

POLYMER COATING

Advanced synthetic polymer sealant delivering exceptional gloss and protection. Perfect for those seeking professional-grade protection at accessible pricing. Excellent UV resistance and easy maintenance. Lasts 12-18 months.

  • ✓ High-Gloss Finish
  • ✓ UV Protection
  • ✓ Water Beading Effect
  • ✓ 12-18 Month Protection
⚡

GRAPHENE COATING

Next-generation graphene-infused coating technology. Superior heat dissipation prevents water spotting, enhanced anti-static properties repel dust, and unparalleled flexibility prevents cracking. The ultimate in modern paint protection.

  • ✓ Superior Heat Resistance
  • ✓ Anti-Static Properties
  • ✓ Self-Cleaning Effect
  • ✓ 5-7 Year Protection
